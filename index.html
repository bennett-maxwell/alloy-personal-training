Franchise Snapshot

Alloy Personal Training is a Georgia-based personal training franchise offering small-group personal training (up to 6 clients per session) delivered by certified instructors, with individualized programs, technology-driven assessments, and accountability coaching. Franchisees operate 1,500-2,000 sq ft facilities, typically in strip centers, under a 10-year franchise agreement with a protected Designated Area of at least 30,000 people. The 2025 FDD reports 77 total outlets (76 franchised, 1 affiliate-owned) across 25 states, with an Item 19 average 2024 gross revenue of $386,914 across 28 mature franchisee facilities.
